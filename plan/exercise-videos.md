# Exercise Demo Videos

Verified reference videos for movements used in the workout plan. Prefer these links when walking through a workout so the demonstrated movement matches the programmed exercise.

## Day 6 mobility

### Couch stretch
- **Programmed:** 45 sec/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Half Kneeling Quad Stretch (Couch Stretch)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p24DLakBhYM
- **Cue:** Keep the torso controlled and squeeze the glute on the kneeling side rather than simply arching the low back.

### Calf stretch
- **Programmed:** 30 sec/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Standing Calf Stretch (Knee Straight)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yZCfQ6YbAvA
- **Cue:** Keep the back knee straight and heel down; the stretch should be felt through the calf.

### 90/90 hip switches
- **Programmed:** 8/side
- **Primary demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — 90/90 Hip Transitions (Hands Supported)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lx07WIkllbA
- **Progression demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — 90/90 Hip Transitions
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-L0s5uSQ-E
- **Cue:** Rotate through the hips under control. Hands-supported is fully acceptable; remove hand support only when the motion stays smooth.

### Thoracic rotations
- **Programmed:** 8/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Quadruped Thoracic Rotation
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADeGAWCDjhQ
- **Cue:** Rotate through the upper back while keeping the movement controlled rather than forcing range through the low back.
