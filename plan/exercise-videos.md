# Exercise Demo Videos

Verified reference videos for movements used in the workout plan. Prefer these links when walking through a workout so the demonstrated movement matches the programmed exercise.

**Verification standard:** confirm that the demo matches the programmed movement and favor established coaching, strength-training, or rehabilitation sources. These videos are references for execution; `plan/workout-plan.md` remains the source of truth for sets, reps, RIR, progression, and substitutions.

## Day 1 — Upper Hybrid Strength + Calisthenics Fundamentals

### Scapular pull-up
- **Programmed:** 6-10 reps
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — The Best Exercise to Increase Pullups
- https://fitnessfaqs.com/video-gallery/the-best-exercise-to-increase-pullups/
- **Cue:** Keep the elbows straight and move from the shoulder blades with slow, controlled depression/retraction. Do not turn it into a partial pull-up.

### Scapular push-up
- **Programmed:** 8-12 reps
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — Unlock Your Shoulders FULL Strength
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HyQw68TSMoA
- **Cue:** Keep the elbows straight and move the shoulder blades through controlled retraction/protraction while keeping the trunk stable.

### Band shoulder pass-through / opener
- **Programmed:** 10 controlled reps
- **Demo:** GMB Fitness / Praxis — Shoulder Dislocates for Shoulder Flexibility
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vP8YmmRMz6I
- **Cue:** Use a wide enough grip for smooth, pain-free motion. Do not force range.

### Thoracic rotation
- **Programmed on Day 1:** 6-8/side
- **Primary demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Quadruped Thoracic Rotation
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADeGAWCDjhQ
- **Alternate verified demo:** Physical Therapy First — Quadruped Thoracic Rotation
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cLEI3hm98PA
- **Cue:** Keep the hips and low back relatively quiet and rotate through the rib cage/mid-back.

### Pull-up progression
- **Programmed:** 4-8 reps
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — How to Pull-Up CORRECTLY (3 Step Guide)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhokcbRLP5w
- **Cue:** Use a comfortable roughly shoulder-width grip, controlled full range of motion, and enough assistance to preserve the programmed RIR.

### Dumbbell or machine chest press
- **Programmed:** 6-10 reps
- **Demo:** Renaissance Periodization — GET MORE RESULTS: Dumbbell Press Master Class
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f0hOKO_eD-Q
- **Timestamp guide:** ~1:00 for dumbbell pressing; ~7:04 for the chest-press machine.
- **Cue:** Use a stable setup and a controlled, comfortable deep range of motion appropriate to the chosen implement.

### Cable row
- **Programmed:** 8-12 reps
- **Demo:** Renaissance Periodization — Seated Cable Row
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UCXxvVItLoM
- **Cue:** Control both the reach and the pull rather than using uncontrolled torso momentum.
- **Note:** A chest-supported row is also allowed by the plan; this demo is specifically for the cable-row option.

### Dip progression
- **Programmed:** 6-10 reps
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — Are You Doing Dips Correctly? (AVOID MISTAKES)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KoS_NMmuxMM
- **Cue:** Use an assistance/support variation when needed, keep the shoulders stable, and use controlled depth.

### Push-up progression
- **Programmed:** 8-15 reps
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — The Perfect Push-Up for Bodyweight Strength
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNjmJmAIIrA
- **Cue:** Maintain a rigid body line, allow normal scapular movement, and use a variation that leaves the programmed RIR.

### Hollow hold
- **Programmed:** 20-40 sec
- **Demo:** FitnessFAQs — The BEST HOLLOW BODY Progressions – Beginner to Advanced
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZqTUwq96iU
- **Cue:** Keep the low back controlled against the floor. Regress the arm/leg position before allowing the lumbar spine to arch.

### Lateral raise
- **Programmed:** 10-20 reps
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Lateral Raise
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Df-rxCiab0Y
- **Cue:** Raise and lower under control and use a load that does not require torso swing.

### Wall slide
- **Programmed:** 10 reps
- **Primary demo:** FitnessFAQs — Instant Pull Up Fix | Do MORE Reps!
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0EPpod2xxo
- **Alternate tutorial:** E3 Rehab — Wall Slide (Tutorial)
- https://vimeo.com/956257193
- **Cue:** Keep the ribs and low back controlled and move only as far as you can maintain the intended arm/wall position.

### Thread-the-needle
- **Programmed:** 8/side
- **Demo:** Physical Therapy First — Thoracic Quadruped Thread the Needle
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cn43k8-7iik
- **Cue:** Keep the hips relatively quiet, rotate through the mid-back, and move smoothly rather than forcing end range.

### Shoulder CARs
- **Programmed:** 5/side
- **Demo:** Progressive Motion — Shoulder CARs (controlled articular rotations)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QRmjqjGlIGI
- **Cue:** Brace the trunk and draw the largest slow, pain-free shoulder circle you can without rib flare or torso rotation.

## Day 6 mobility

### Couch stretch
- **Programmed:** 45 sec/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Half Kneeling Quad Stretch (Couch Stretch)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p24DLakBhYM
- **Cue:** Keep the torso controlled and squeeze the glute on the kneeling side rather than simply arching the low back.

### Calf stretch
- **Programmed:** 30 sec/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Standing Calf Stretch (Knee Straight)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yZCfQ6YbAvA
- **Cue:** Keep the back knee straight and heel down; the stretch should be felt through the calf.

### 90/90 hip switches
- **Programmed:** 8/side
- **Primary demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — 90/90 Hip Transitions (Hands Supported)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lx07WIkllbA
- **Progression demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — 90/90 Hip Transitions
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-L0s5uSQ-E
- **Cue:** Rotate through the hips under control. Hands-supported is fully acceptable; remove hand support only when the motion stays smooth.

### Thoracic rotations
- **Programmed:** 8/side
- **Demo:** E3 Rehab Exercise Library — Quadruped Thoracic Rotation
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADeGAWCDjhQ
- **Cue:** Rotate through the upper back while keeping the movement controlled rather than forcing range through the low back.
