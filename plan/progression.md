# Progression index for the active v3 plan

The current exercise choices, sets, home fallbacks, and progression tracks are in workout-plan.md. Use its "Calisthenics progression tracks," "Gym strength progression," and "Progression and recovery rules" sections as the source of truth. This file is a quick review aid, not a second workout prescription.

## Strength and skills

- Record the variation, load or assistance, reps or hold time, and reps in reserve (RIR) for a few key movements.
- Normal working sets use about 1-2 RIR; during a restart use about 2-3 RIR or remove a working set. Stop skill sets before technique breaks down.
- Change one progression variable at a time: reps, load, assistance, range of motion, leverage, or hold time.
- The active markers are push-up, pull-up, dip, pike/HSPU, handstand, L-sit, unilateral squat, chest press, row, squat/leg press, and RDL. Details and optional future branches are in workout-plan.md.
- Record recurring pain or unusual tension. Low-left-back/lat symptoms appear in the 2026-08-02, 2026-08-13, 2026-08-27, and 2026-09-22 logs; the last lower session stopped RDLs when tension returned. Review current symptoms before choosing the next hinge load or variation.

## Running and mobility

- Follow the next scheduled Just Run: Zero to 5K session for each of the three running days. Record completion, time or distance if available, effort, and recovery.
- Do not add the old interval or tempo progression from the superseded plan while following Just Run.
- For mobility, compare repeatable notes such as deep squat comfort, knee-to-wall range, 90/90 control, shoulder position, and wrist comfort.

## Weekly review

Compare performance and recovery over several sessions, not one workout. If several markers worsen together, review sleep, calorie intake, running fatigue, and hard-set volume before adding work. Use tracking/weekly-checkin.md for the decision and record the evidence behind it.
